# QForm Simulation

This folder contains the 2D and 3D QForm simulations performed for the C45 steel forging-process analysis.

The simulations were used to evaluate:

- Material flow
- Flash formation
- Forging defects
- Stress distribution
- Strain distribution
- Workpiece temperature
- Tool temperature
- Billet-dimension optimization
- Lubrication effects

## Simulation Stages

### 2D Simulation

The initial 2D simulation revealed defects related to the billet dimensions and flash geometry.

The billet geometry was then modified by reducing its height and increasing its diameter, and the simulation was repeated.

### 3D Simulation

A 3D QForm simulation was performed to evaluate the complete forging process and its thermal behavior.

The simulation results were also used to study the effect of lubricant selection on the temperatures of the workpiece and forging tools.
