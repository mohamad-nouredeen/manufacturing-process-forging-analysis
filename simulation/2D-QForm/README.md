# 2D QForm Simulation

This folder contains the 2D QForm simulations performed during the development and optimization of the C45 steel forging process.

## Analysis

The initial simulation was used to evaluate material flow and identify potential defects in the workpiece.

The results indicated that the initial billet geometry was not optimal. The billet dimensions were therefore modified by increasing the diameter and reducing the height before repeating the simulation.

The updated simulation was then evaluated in terms of:

- Material flow
- Flash formation
- Stress distribution
- Strain distribution
- Final workpiece filling
